# Design review

Use the checks appropriate to the artifact's actual purpose. These are review questions, not a required set of sections to add to every deliverable.

## Before scaling the design

Establish the reader's main jobs and the meaningful content unit. Choose a representative demanding example before laying out a long series. Useful stress cases include a long name with metadata, a multi-part group, a portrait image, a dense comparison, a long source description, or a page that must leave writing room. Test only the cases present in the assignment.

Decide what the recurring components mean before assigning their appearance. A small palette can reinforce identity, decision values, actions, consequential notes, and evidence. Different roles should be recognizable through labels, hierarchy, and spacing as well as color.

## Review by reader question

| Reader question | What to inspect | Repair the underlying problem |
|---|---|---|
| What is this, and why am I reading it? | Title, useful finding or purpose; no dominant internal status code. | Reorder identity, finding, metadata, and action roles. |
| What belongs together? | Correspondence between pictures, names, included pieces, and notes. | Change the presentation unit or grouping. |
| What is established? | Clear separation of facts, assumptions, unknown identity, absence, and checks. | Reconcile the information model and wording. |
| What needs doing? | Actionable, item-specific steps in the appropriate place. | Move commands out of headings and factual tables; use checkboxes where progress tracking helps. |
| What does this number mean? | Unit, quantity, period, condition, scenario, and comparison basis. | Make the basis explicit in the data and its display. |
| How do these options compare? | Parallel fields, labels, units, and treatments. | Standardize the comparison component and subordinate alternatives. |
| Can I recognize the object? | Useful close-up, position cue, actual/reference distinction. | Replace vague description or decorative imagery with recognition evidence. |
| Where did this claim come from? | Useful inline link, anchored note, or named supporting source. | Connect the claim to evidence and explain the source's role. |
| Can I find an entry again? | Useful labels, a compact key if needed, subordinate identifiers. | Simplify metadata and provide an appropriate lookup structure. |
| Can I read and use this here? | Final-scale legibility, viewing distance, device width, writing space. | Adjust composition and density for the use environment. |

## Render and interaction checks

For paged output, verify actual dimensions and orientation, font faces and embedding where relevant, page breaks, group continuity, header/footer clearance, table wrapping, and unclipped values. A source-code dimension or a text extraction is insufficient evidence of visual quality.

Inspect optical spacing: interior padding, gaps between sections, text baselines, pill alignment, captions, and the space after the final item in a card. A mathematically centered box may still look misaligned because of font metrics.

Check the entire relevant reading unit. A moved paragraph can produce an orphaned note or a references-only page; a changed number can wrap a card or alter its emphasis. Keep complete groups together when practical, and use a deliberate continuation when the content warrants more space.

For a digital artifact, check useful widths, readable wrapping, links, and requested controls. Test the actual destination format. A drawing of a checkbox can be appropriate on paper; a claim of editable or persistent state requires working controls in that medium.

## Reconciliation and finish

Check that changed facts reach all affected representations, including summaries, contents, captions, uncertainty labels, actions, drafts, quantities, alternatives, and totals. Prevent shared constituents or alternative sale routes from being counted twice.

Review the artifact cold, using the same questions its reader will bring. Remove author-facing notes and repeated labels that do not help. Keep the short practical implication near the relevant decision; place substantial supporting analysis in a useful appendix.

After substantive pattern changes, inspect the other instances of the pattern. After a narrow edit, inspect affected output and its downstream dependencies. Once the required checks pass, continue to delivery unless new changes or unresolved concerns justify another pass. Report concrete verification rather than an unsupported claim of perfection.
